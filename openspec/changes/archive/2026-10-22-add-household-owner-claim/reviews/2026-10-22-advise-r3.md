## Blind pass (written from the proposal, the household-authority delta, and `rc.wps/00-mjolnir-wan-admin` + `mjolnir-wan-admin`, before opening design.md / tasks.md / spec delta)

1. **Pin** a symmetric construction the node can run: it stores a derived key `k` from the card secret, and the client MACs the claim under `k`. A one-way hash of the secret can't check a MAC keyed by the secret. State that `k` is secret-equivalent.
2. **Pin** what the MAC covers, all fixed-width: owner pk, full node id, epoch, and a node-issued nonce that is fresh per attempt. Compare in constant time in meshd.
3. **Pin** that the claim client is installed code that the node doesn't serve (Lightning Admin). On an open AP, a page served by the entry router can be swapped, so it must not accept the card secret.
4. **Pin** that the client checks the node signature against the full node id from the card before it sends the MAC. The client should also accept only a node-signed outcome, so a relay can't fake "claimed".
5. **Pin** button dispatch for each state: unclaimed with a verifier arms the ceremony only; no verifier keeps today's WAN-SSH toggle exactly; owned toggles WAN SSH unless a recovery has been credential-verified and is pending.
6. **Pin** one durable CAS from unclaimed to epoch 1 in meshd. hello and rc.wps only relay or signal.
7. **Pin** one-time use: a successful claim retires `k`, so neither a stolen card nor a later flash read of an owned node gives a forging key. A reset wipes the binding and `k` and does not re-arm the card.
8. **Pin** recovery: both epochs advance, prior recovery creds are retired, and a forked head fails closed for privileged ops while forwarding stays up.
9. **Refuse**: button-only, secret-only, WPS as presence, a hub page with secret entry, and a no-verifier node that loses WAN SSH or arms a ceremony that can never complete.
10. **Tradeoff**: the attempt cap lets an on-path neighbor burn the window. That is acceptable as DoS-only. The alternative, no cap, gives online guessing inside the window.

## Compare

**B1 (verifier can check the HMAC): answered.** design.md §Ceremony 2 and lines
36–37 now read: `k = HMAC-SHA256(salt, secret)`. The node stores `k`, and the
proof is `HMAC-SHA256(k, owner pk ‖ node id ‖ epoch ‖ nonce)` with the nonce
taken from the node-signed challenge. meshd can recompute and compare that, so
the construction closes. `k` is stated to be secret-equivalent for forging,
and a flash read counts as physical compromise. The entropy floor stays at
≥ 80 bits. This matches blind 1–2.

**B2 (claim client is Lightning Admin): answered.** design.md lines 38–40:
"Card entry, owner key generation, and the HMAC run only in Lightning Admin.
hello.mesh may point at that app. It has no secret field." The spec delta
scenario is now "Hub offers no secret entry": the page doesn't accept the card
secret, and hub handlers don't receive it. The design still says the client
checks the node signature against the card's full node id before it sends
anything (lines 16 and 31–34, which also closes r2 N3). This matches blind 3–4.

**B3 (no verifier keeps the WAN-SSH toggle): answered.** design.md lines 49–54
spell out three states. With a verifier present and the node unclaimed, or
with a pending recovery whose credential has already verified (closing r2 N2),
the press arms the ceremony only. With no verifier, the press "keeps the
existing WAN-SSH toggle". On an owned node with no verified pending recovery,
the press toggles WAN SSH only. The spec delta adds the scenario "No verifier
keeps the WAN toggle". This matches blind 5 and 9.

r2 non-blocking items: N2, N3 and N4 are answered. N1 (factor wording) is
partly answered: lines 31–34 are honest, but lines 8–11 still list device
proof as factor 1. N5 (which button resets) is open. Both are carried below.

Steelman against my take: blind 4b (a node-signed outcome) and blind 7
(retiring `k` on success) are not in the design. Neither is a takeover path.
A faked "claimed" outcome only misleads the purchaser until their first signed
operation fails. A retained `k` can't rebind an owned node, because the CAS
runs only from unclaimed and dispatch on an owned node never arms a claim
ceremony. These are hardening items, not acceptance gates. The packet's three
gates hold in design.md and the spec delta.

### Carry-forwards (non-blocking; fix before or during implementation, and before fold)

- **C1. tasks.md line 11 is stale against the design.** It reads "unclaimed or
  pending recovery arms the ceremony only; otherwise the existing WAN-SSH
  toggle". That is the two-state rule r2 B3 rejected. It needs "verifier
  present and unclaimed, or a pending recovery whose credential has verified".
  Line 12's test list still lacks the no-verifier case and the
  unverified-pending-recovery case. The spec scenario covers the first one, but
  the implementer's checklist should too. The reader did not edit these lines.
  They are the author's.
- **C2. The dispatch scenarios have no SHALL requirement to sit under.** "No
  verifier keeps the WAN toggle" is filed under "The hub does not receive owner
  secrets", and "Ceremony press does not open WAN SSH" is under the claim
  requirement. Add a "Setup button dispatch" requirement with SHALL text for
  the three states, and move both scenarios under it. Otherwise the folded
  living spec carries a behavior that only a scenario states.
- **C3. Signed window expiry vs request-then-press.** §Ceremony 1 says the node
  signs "window expiry" into the challenge. But order is request, then press,
  so the window doesn't exist yet when the challenge is signed. Either sign a
  request expiry, or have the node sign a second, post-press confirmation.
- **C4. Pin the salt source.** The design leaves "printed on the card or
  carried in the node-signed challenge" open. Pick one so Lightning Admin and
  the provisioning image agree. Pin a fixed-width encoding for the MAC input,
  including how "unclaimed" encodes as an epoch, and a constant-time compare.
- **C5. Retire `k` on successful claim** (blind 7). That makes "one-time" in the
  proposal literal.
- **C6. Node-signed claim outcome** (blind 4b). The 5 s relay wait means the
  client learns the result after the press. Have it accept only a node-signed
  outcome over the same request.
- **C7. The spec delta's recovery requirement omits the epoch advance.** It says
  only that credentials retire. Mirror design.md line 70 there: both the
  ownership and the authority epoch advance. Also align the spec line 6 wording,
  "HMAC … under the card secret", to "under the key derived from the card
  secret".
- **C8. r2 N1 and N5 remain.** Reword design lines 8–11 as two node-side factors
  plus one client-side check. Name the button or action that performs the
  destructive reset, and say that the house sees that node as lost.

## Verdict

**Accept.** The stored `k` is the MAC key, so meshd can check the proof. Card
entry, keygen and the HMAC are confined to Lightning Admin, and the hub page
has no secret field. A node with no verifier keeps today's WAN-SSH toggle and
never arms a ceremony. C1–C8 are editorial or hardening work, and none of
them reopens a settled decision. C1 and C2 should land before the dispatch
task is built.
