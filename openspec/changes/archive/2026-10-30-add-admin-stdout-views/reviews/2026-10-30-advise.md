## Comparison and steelman

The author answered the central concerns: the main window remains, discovery stays scan_link_local, the host command has a fixed absolute path and no extra arguments, unknown sources are refused, no shell is involved, output is text rather than HTML, and no remote-node source or shared Papyrus crate is introduced. Copying chrome and retaining raw output are proportionate choices for the first source. A generic process framework, structured NIC parser, and 3D engine would not improve this landing's contract.

Against my blind take, separate stderr presentation is not inherently necessary: a clearly labeled combined transcript could work. Likewise, detailed timing constants and Rust/IPC types can belong to the pipe child. However, the parent must establish the lifecycle and observable-result invariants that both children depend on. The declared chrome spike is the right place to establish actual macOS window behavior; its absence today is not itself a reason to reject the architecture.

## Owed corrections

1. **Define the process-to-well boundary and its lifetime.** [design.md:39](../design.md#L39) leaves stderr merged or separate, and [design.md:55](../design.md#L55) promises only visible truncation and a bounded poll. The [stdout requirement](../specs/admin-stdout-views/spec.md#L57) carries neither capture bounds nor a lifetime rule. Display truncation alone permits unbounded capture before rendering; bounded polling alone permits overlapping hung children. Destroying the window currently says nothing about its running process. Pin finite execution and capture bounds, timeout/cancellation with child termination and reaping, and prevention of overlapping or stale runs. Choose stdout/stderr/exit-status semantics and distinguish loading, successful empty output, failure, and truncation. State unsupported-host behavior without adding another source. Concrete constants and transport types can be owned by add-host-ifconfig-view, but the contract must require them and include failure/lifecycle scenarios. A one-shot first landing is sufficient; this does not require polling or a refresh UI.

2. **Resolve the resize gesture contradiction.** [spec delta:40–43](../specs/admin-stdout-views/spec.md#L40) makes Option/Alt-drag resize toward the bottom-right, then makes held Option resize from the center. These describe the same input with different anchors. Specify distinct inputs or one anchor, including precedence when Control, Option, and Shift combine, and add resize scenarios. The child should not have to invent the gesture contract while copying chrome.

3. **Keep multiple wells in the authorized later landing.** The packet defers many wells; [proposal.md:25](../proposal.md#L25) and its journey do too. But [spec delta:98](../specs/admin-stdout-views/spec.md#L98) unconditionally requires simultaneous canvases and a two-window scenario. The strongest defense is that two independent overlays are simpler than arranging a grid. That distinction is technically reasonable but does not match this packet's scope, and it creates a fold obligation beyond the stated first landing. Defer the multi-canvas SHALL/scenario to its later change and keep this contract foldable after the single canvas and ifconfig children land.
