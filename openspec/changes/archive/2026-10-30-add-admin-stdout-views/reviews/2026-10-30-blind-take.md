# Independent blind take

Written before opening design.md, tasks.md, or the proposed spec delta; based on the packet Why, cited code, and living specs.

1. Pin the distinction between the workstation's own NIC stdout and the existing structured neighbor discovery scan.
2. Preserve the decorated main window and its discovery, network-name apply, and guild-signing responsibilities.
3. Pin backend-owned source identifiers to absolute executables and fixed argv; first source is /sbin/ifconfig with no arguments.
4. Refuse shell strings, caller-selected executable paths or arguments, and any implicit radio or guild mutation.
5. Treat stdout as inert text, preserving its useful formatting without interpreting markup or terminal controls as commands.
6. Pin observable loading, empty, successful, and failed outcomes; distinguish stderr and exit status from stdout.
7. Require bounded execution and capture, with explicit timeout, refresh-overlap, and window-close cleanup behavior.
8. Require the chrome landing to prove macOS transparency, drag, close, and usable text scrolling without breaking the main window.
9. Refuse a grid, arbitrary command console, or replacement discovery implementation in this contract's first landing.
10. Tradeoff: raw ifconfig gives familiar diagnostic fidelity at the cost of portable structured semantics; name the macOS scope and unsupported-host behavior.
