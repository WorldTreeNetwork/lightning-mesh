## Independent take

1. Pin one authoritative radioset for radio assignments; wired profiles may constrain compatibility but must not regenerate a second assignment map.
2. Pin the OpenWrt One default to 5 GHz mesh and 2.4 GHz client AP, with Ethernet WAN governed by the wired profile.
3. Resolve selectors against actual radio inventory and refuse absent or ambiguous matches; band discovery must never silently select the last device.
4. Enforce one active role per physical radio, with omitted devices explicitly accounted for and no accidental mesh/AP concurrency.
5. Refuse station takeover without uplink ownership; preserve an owned station and its candidate-bound configuration during unrelated projection.
6. Keep live station application gated on adapter health and restoration capability; accepting a pure plan must not imply live support or downstream internet.
7. Bind resolved assignments and their identity to the same revision-checked durable transaction as projected UCI, so reapply and rollback cannot drift.
8. Retain explicit acknowledgement before isolating wireless-only peers, plus reserved-mesh and management/mesh reachability safeguards.
9. Preserve routed client domains, house-gateway exports, recovery-address restrictions, and secret exclusion from public revisions and receipts.
10. Tradeoff: deterministic refusal on uncertain hardware mapping limits automatic provisioning, but is preferable to silently moving backhaul or station service.

## Comparison and steelman

The revised contract answers the concerns above without adding another radio assignment store. Wired profiles supply ports and may reference a radioset id; the resolved radio functions belong to `mjolnir.radioset`. Hardware maps supply identity, not function assignments. Client SSID and encryption remain in `mjolnir.radio`. Unchanged living requirements retain gateway-export, recovery-address, credential, and transaction safeguards.

The strongest case against my blind take is that strict identity matching and a committed resolved table cost more than selecting radios by band at each apply. Here that cost is justified: same-band devices are not interchangeable, and replay must preserve a committed selection after the selecting environment disappears. The author also reasonably distinguishes mapped-but-omitted devices (disabled) from unmapped devices belonging to the dongle owner (unmanaged, with a managed-interface collision refused). That is a bounded ownership rule, not an incomplete role assignment.
