## Independent take (written before opening design.md or tasks.md)

1. Pin reciprocal consent between two full guild ids; either house's signature alone must admit nothing.
2. Pin signer authority to authenticated house history, because sign_guild accepts a caller-selected guild id.
3. Refuse guild_view::apply_op as an authority source: a verified signature does not authorize its roster replacement.
4. Pin the authority bootstrap, authorized roster transitions, and the point in history at which signer eligibility is evaluated.
5. Bind the signed envelope target_fp to the issuing house and bind the body to that same house and its intended peer.
6. Recompute each permitted /48 from its full guild id; refuse arbitrary, stock, legacy, self, or collision-ambiguous prefixes.
7. Pin operation kind, canonical signed fields, and reciprocal matching so unrelated signatures cannot complete a permit.
8. Pin deterministic replay, revocation, regrant, and malformed-record handling so stale consent cannot silently reappear.
9. Keep admission as explicit derived state, with no route, address, radio, or Babel mutation in this capability.
10. Tradeoff: strict authority validation may withhold connectivity for incomplete history; fail closed rather than infer control from display state.

## Comparison and verdict

Both blockers from the previous review are answered in [design.md](../design.md) and the [foreign-prefix-permit delta](../specs/foreign-prefix-permit/spec.md).

- **Signer authority is pinned.** The authority input maps full guild ids to actor public keys and roles; only an `admin` entry for the admitting guild counts. Evaluation occurs at predicate time. Missing snapshots, missing guild entries, unrelated actors, and non-admin roles fail closed. The snapshot is explicitly trusted caller input and explicitly cannot come from the guild-log projection. This answers the `sign_guild` caller-selected-id and `guild_view::apply_op` unauthenticated-roster counterexamples without claiming that either existing function authenticates house control.
- **Target binding is pinned.** Both documents require a present `target_fp` equal to body `keyspace`; exact 32/32/6-byte fields and reciprocal full guild identities remain required. The negative scenario uses an authorized signer, so authority failure cannot mask a target-binding failure.
- **The acceptance cases distinguish authorization from signature validity.** Actor `ee` signing both correct directions is rejected, a `member` is rejected, absent authority is rejected, and the authorized reciprocal fixture succeeds. One direction, self-pairs, and non-derived prefixes fail. The predicate accepts only the named operation kind, preserves full identities in its result, and has no networking side effects.
- **The Admin delta preserves the existing contract.** The renamed protocol-kinds requirement retains join, assign/nest, and no-rename scenarios. The added kind must leave roster, profile, epoch, and assigned nodes unchanged and must not break log inspection or write the radio configuration.

No blocking contract gap remains. The task list reflects the amended predicate and negative cases; only its Fresh advise marker is checked by this review.

## Steelman against the independent take

My blind take preferred authenticated history reconstruction and explicit revocation handling. The stronger argument for the author's narrower boundary is that this node implements a pure evidence predicate with trusted authority supplied separately. Deriving or transporting that authority would add a second subsystem without improving the predicate's specified checks. The caller remains responsible for authenticating the snapshot; accepting this contract does not certify that any existing caller supplies it safely.

Predicate-time authority is a deliberate semantic choice: historical consent counts only while its signer is an admin in the supplied snapshot. There is no signing-time authorization guarantee, expiry, withdrawal, or revocation tombstone. Re-presented evidence can count again when current authority permits it. Those limits are explicit, so no replay state machine is required for this node.

I also withdraw collision rejection as a prerequisite: the living ULA spec leaves collisions undetected, and the amendment explicitly retains full guild identity rather than equating it with the /48. Routing consumers must honor that limitation. Babel export and Admin TUN routing remain outside this acceptance. The unverified-hypotheses section says none; the skipped spike is optional and need not precede implementation of this bounded contract.
