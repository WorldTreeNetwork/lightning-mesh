## Blind pass

1. Pin a non-zero exit when node A retains the STA's proto 158 `/32` after the hop, even when IPv4 continuity succeeds.
2. Pin the pre-hop invariant that this STA `/32` is absent on both nodes, so the test proves a move rather than inheriting stale state.
3. Refuse any xfail, warning-only, or partial-green treatment for dual post-hop proto 158 routes.
4. Refuse every path that lets simulated evidence close `5wc`, `wvg`, `wvg.1`, or `sz9.1`.
5. Require a real association transition: present on A only before, present on B only after.
6. Require exact route evidence for the STA `/32`; substring grep or any proto 158 route is too weak for an instrument gate.
7. Require LeaseBook's current LWW MAC-to-IP mapping to equal the baseline rather than merely logging state text.
8. Require sequenced egress recovery to be attributed to node B; gateway ping counts alone do not establish the egress node.
9. Preserve ordered observations and failure diagnostics so a red dual-route run still records the successful keep-IPv4 sub-result.
10. The tradeoff is deliberately blocking a green e2e result on the known meshd withdrawal defect, gaining an honest gate at the cost of no passing happy-path run until `sz9` is fixed.

## Comparison and steelman

The author answered the blocking concern directly and at the normative layer. The steer record says dual proto 158 is FAIL rather than xfail; the proposal repeats that decision; and the spec delta requires a non-zero exit whenever A retains the STA `/32`, even when B also has it. This preserves the useful observation that IPv4 stayed constant without misreporting the end-to-end contract as green.

The strongest case for the author's deliberately thin design is that `deploy/sim/e2e/run.sh keep-ip` wraps `roam-keep-ip.sh` instead of creating a second hop mechanism. The living spec already owns the broader association, LeaseBook, route-move, and egress observations. Reusing that instrument keeps the new runner a stable entry point and avoids divergent meanings of “keep-ip.”

The authoritative delta preserves the full metal boundary: it prohibits closing `5wc`, `wvg`, `wvg.1`, and `sz9.1`, and explicitly rejects expected-fail treatment. The shorter implementation-task wording omits `wvg.1`, but it does not weaken the normative requirement; act and verification must follow the delta's complete list.

The intentional tradeoff matches the blind pass: this e2e target remains red until meshd withdraws A's stale route. That is the correct instrument behavior, while the successful keep-IPv4 observation remains diagnostic evidence rather than a waiver.
