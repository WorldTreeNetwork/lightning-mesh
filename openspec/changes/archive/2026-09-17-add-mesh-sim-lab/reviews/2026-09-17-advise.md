## Blind pass

1. Pin the capability as a reproducible OpenWrt control/dataplane lab on named x86_64 KVM guests, never as Cudy or Filogic emulation.
2. Pin the product invariant: the L3 overlay and per-node routed client prefixes remain under test; vwifi is replaceable radio plumbing.
3. Refuse any topology that bridges client L2 between nodes, because keep-IP is proxy-ARP plus protocol-158 mobility `/32`s, not a shared broadcast domain.
4. Separate backhaul air, Ethernet/NAT, and out-of-band management so harness reachability cannot accidentally satisfy dataplane assertions.
5. Require a distinct x86_64-musl `meshd` artifact and image path; the existing aarch64/mt76 deployment artifacts are evidence about metal, not guest inputs.
6. Require the simulation install/profile path to fail closed on identifiable Cudy boards and avoid reusing metal fleet/radio configuration by default.
7. Reuse the proven named-libvirt-domain and SSH `BatchMode` lifecycle pattern, including deterministic boot discovery, idempotent setup, and cleanup.
8. Define evidence labels so a green simulation proves only the named virtual contract and cannot close hardware RF, live-fleet, or walking-phone validation.
9. Make the first instrument a Linux STA hop A→B that observes retained address, proxy ARP, protocol-158 `/32`, babel export, and bounded disruption without claiming iPhone behavior.
10. Accept lower RF/driver fidelity for fast reproducibility, provided the contract explicitly trades away mt76/Filogic, real roaming heuristics, and household NAT proof until later fixtures or metal tests.

## Comparison and steelman

The proposed architecture answers most of that take directly. It keeps the L3 overlay as the product, uses generic OpenWrt rather than pretending to emulate Filogic, creates a host-ISA musl artifact, separates vwifi from virtio Ethernet, reuses named libvirt/BatchMode operations, and draws the evidence boundary around Linux STA simulation instead of iPhone or metal validation. The target topology and incremental slice order are coherent. Keeping the existing netns spike as a lower-level overlay test while making this lab exercise procd, UCI, `mjolnir-apply`, `wpad-mesh`, and `iw` is the strongest reason to add the capability.

## Blocking corrections

1. **The sim-profile guard is a deny-list, not a sim-only gate.** `design.md:22-24` and the delta at `specs/mesh-sim-lab/spec.md:54-65` promise refusal only for Cudy/Filogic. The packet's false condition is broader: a sim profile must not apply on metal. Supported metal also includes non-Cudy OpenWrt targets, and future hardware would bypass a Cudy block-list. Require positive identification of the intended virtual guest (with all checks completed before the first UCI/wireless write), plus a scenario proving that non-Cudy physical OpenWrt is refused.

2. **“Mgmt is separate” is not yet a falsifiable dataplane boundary.** The shared management network carries SSH and vwifi TCP, but the delta does not forbid direct guest-to-guest babel/iroh/overlay traffic over that interface. Current LAN-mode `meshd` pins iroh to the derived address on the configured backhaul (`mjolnir-meshd.rs:395-440`), but the lab contract must preserve that property rather than rely on an implementation accident. Add a negative-control scenario: with mgmt SSH still healthy, removing or partitioning vwifi/backhaul reachability must remove babel adjacency and peer `10.254` reachability. A green harness must therefore be impossible through the management NIC alone.

3. **The daily guest machine model is misstated.** `design.md:7` says QEMU `-M virt` / x86_64, but this host's `qemu-system-x86_64 -machine help` offers PC/q35-class machines and no `virt`; `virt` is an aarch64 machine. Pin daily x86_64 KVM to an x86 PC/q35 libvirt machine and reserve `-M virt` for the optional `armsr` aarch64 smoke guest.

After those three contract fixes, the architecture should be ready for another independent advise pass. No implementation is requested by this review.
