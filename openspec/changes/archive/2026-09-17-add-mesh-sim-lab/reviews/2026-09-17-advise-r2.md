## Blind pass: independent take

1. Pin the daily target to x86_64 OpenWrt guests on the existing Linux KVM/libvirt host; this is systems virtualization, not MediaTek SoC emulation.
2. Pin the lab's proof boundary to OpenWrt service, routing, vwifi, and harness behavior; real mt76 RF and phone behavior remain metal evidence.
3. Require an affirmative simulation-guest identity before any simulated radio profile may mutate wireless state.
4. Refuse that profile on every unmarked host, including non-Cudy metal, and require the refusal to occur before the first wireless write.
5. Keep Ethernet NAT, vwifi air, and management transport separate enough that each failure plane can be isolated and observed.
6. Require a negative control that partitions vwifi while management SSH stays available and babel plus `10.254` overlay reachability fail.
7. Treat overlay survival through the management network as a topology leak and a failed fixture, not as resilience.
8. Refuse Filogic sysupgrade boot claims, green-lab closure of hardware/phone beads, and Linux-STA results presented as iPhone DNAv4 proof.
9. Reuse named libvirt domains and `BatchMode=yes` operational discipline, while keeping this lab under `deploy/sim/` rather than the live OpenWrt deployment surface.
10. Accept the tradeoff that deterministic, cheap daily coverage gains observability and repeatability at the cost of real-RF fidelity, which remains a later armsr/metal smoke concern.

## Comparison and steelman

The amended design answers the blind take rather than replacing it with a competing lab. It keeps the L3 overlay under test and the radio replaceable, exercises OpenWrt seams the existing netns spike cannot exercise, separates the simulation tree from live fleet rollout, and bounds the evidence to Linux-STA and virtual-fixture claims. The explicit residual list preserves the important costs: hwsim is not mt76, virtio is not DSA, and Linux is not iPhone DNAv4.

The strongest case for carrying vwifi TCP on the management network is operational: vwifi needs an out-of-band relay/control path, and a separate SSH path keeps a broken air fixture diagnosable. The amended contract makes that convenience safe by turning isolation into an observable negative control rather than trusting interface naming. If SSH survives while babel adjacency and peer `10.254` die after a vwifi partition, the fixture demonstrates that management is control transport, not an accidental alternate mesh.
