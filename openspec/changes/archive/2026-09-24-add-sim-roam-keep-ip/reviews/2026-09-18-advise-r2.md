## Blind pass

1. Pin an ordered pre-hop baseline: STA associated on A, its IPv4/MAC, LeaseBook mapping, proto-158 route ownership, and observable egress through A.
2. Pin the requested roam command as the sole transition stimulus and require post-command station evidence that the same STA moved A→B.
3. Pin keep-IP as byte-for-byte equality of the STA IPv4 before and after the association transition, not merely renewed connectivity.
4. Pin route exclusivity after convergence: the guest /32 with protocol 158 exists on B and is absent from A.
5. Pin LeaseBook convergence as the same MAC→IP winning entry on both nodes under the shipped LWW rule, with enough version evidence to detect divergence.
6. Pin egress continuity with a monotonically sequenced probe stream, timestamps, explicit A/B egress-node observations, and computed loss/gap across the hop.
7. Require a property-red fixture where the command does not produce the transition, A retains the /32, leases diverge, and traffic never resumes through B; the harness must fail.
8. Require a stable evidence schema naming the hop mechanism and carrying station/neighbour facts, route protocol/location, lease entries, probe sequence/loss, timestamps, and egress node.
9. Refuse shared-L2 stitching, workstation SSID attachment, or any claim that sim green closes 5wc, wvg, wvg.1, or sz9.1.
10. Accept deterministic vwifi/wpa_cli control over client-realistic roaming as the instrument tradeoff, provided the log distinguishes commanded mechanism from observed association and dataplane outcomes.

## Comparison and steelman

The added design and delta answer the blind-pass concerns without expanding the instrument into a claim about phone or metal behavior. The STA-only fixture is from the same sim image, the command is distinguished from the observed transition, and the design uses the real route inputs—station association plus usable neighbour state—before treating the B-side mobility route as expected. Forced `wpa_cli roam` or controlled RSSI movement is therefore a sound repeatability tradeoff for this lab instrument, while the explicit evidence boundary preserves the still-open metal work.

The author also chose the stricter useful interpretation of “keep IP”: a byte-stable address is necessary but not sufficient. Green additionally requires route exclusivity, current LWW MAC→IP continuity, proxy-ARP reachability, and resumed egress through B. This is stronger than simply proving that the roam command returned successfully or that one post-hop ping happened to work.

## R2 closure

1. The ordered baseline and green contract are closed. Baseline captures MAC, IPv4, home node, LWW entry with HLC, A-present/B-absent station state, usable neighbour state, route state, and an already-running probe. Green then requires observed A→B association, unchanged IPv4, proto-158 `/32` on B only and absent on A, the same current-LWW MAC→IP mapping, and timestamped/sequenced recovery through B with loss accounting.
2. Property-red is closed normatively in the delta: command without transition, stale `/32` on A, lease divergence, and no resumed success through B each require a non-zero harness exit. The design additionally requires CI fixtures or transcripts so these are detector tests rather than live-hop aspirations.
3. The evidence contract is closed. The log names `wpa_cli-roam` versus `vwifi-ctrl` and carries station/neighbour evidence, pre/post route ownership, lease value/version evidence, cadence and probe sequence/loss, timing points, and the observed post-hop egress node.

The no-shared-L2 rule and the prohibition on closing `5wc`, `wvg`, `wvg.1`, or `sz9.1` remain intact. No new blocker emerged.
