## Blind pass

1. Pin the instrument to the STA-only OpenWrt guest, the same sim image lineage, and a controlled A-to-B association transition rather than inferred radio movement.
2. Pin a pre-hop baseline containing the STA MAC, vended IPv4, home node, LeaseBook entry, association on A, and the absence of a foreign mobility route on B.
3. Require `iw station dump` association plus usable `ip neigh` evidence before expecting the new node to install the mobility route, matching the real `guest_routes` inputs.
4. Record the proto-158 `/32` as a move, not merely an appearance: it must be absent from A and present on B only after the hop.
5. Record the current LeaseBook LWW entry and prove that the same MAC still maps to the same IPv4; do not model a first-write-only lease rule.
6. Measure egress with a timestamped or sequenced continuous probe across the hop, reporting the last success, first resumed success, loss count, and observed new-node egress.
7. Pin proxy-ARP reachability as part of the dataplane observation, because keep-IP without reaching the home gateway is not a successful roam.
8. Refuse any implementation that stitches client L2 or makes a shared broadcast domain a prerequisite; the household path is routed `/24`s plus proxy-ARP and mobility `/32`.
9. Refuse a green result based only on issuing `vwifi-ctrl` or `wpa_cli roam`; success requires observed B association, A disassociation, route movement, stable lease, and resumed traffic.
10. Tradeoff: forced roam control improves determinism and repeatability but weakens radio/client realism, so expose the mechanism and timing while reserving all iPhone, mt76, and metal conclusions.

## Comparison and steelman

The proposal is directionally sound and unusually disciplined about evidence scope. It chooses the correct STA-only guest, keeps the existing image lineage, names deterministic hop controls, asks for the four observations already promised by the living spec, says the `/32` is on B only, and explicitly prohibits closing the metal roam beads. The delta also preserves the most important epistemic boundary: a Linux/hwsim pass is not iPhone/mt76 proof. Nothing in the change introduces shared L2, and the CLI-log surface is proportionate to a first lab instrument.

Against the blind take, the author answered the fixture, broad route-location intent, lease observation, gap observation, hop-control tradeoff, and metal-evidence refusal. Those choices are worth keeping.

## Blocking notes

The change does not yet define a trustworthy green result. There is no `design.md`; the proposal, delta, and tasks therefore carry the whole measurement contract, but they say only that the harness “records” or “prints” four observations. They do not require an observed A→B station transition, the `iw station dump` plus usable-neighbour preconditions used by `guest_routes`, removal of the proto-158 `/32` from A, or an unchanged *current LWW* LeaseBook mapping. Issuing a roam command and seeing a route appear on B could therefore pass while the STA never moved cleanly, A still advertises a stale host route, or the lease state diverged.

“Egress gap” is also undefined: there is no probe cadence, monotonic timing or sequence/loss accounting, resumed-success condition, or proof that post-hop egress is B. Without that contract, a single before/after ping cannot distinguish a bounded roam gap from an outage, and it does not exercise the proxy-ARP path strongly enough to support keep-IP evidence.

Finally, rigor is `instrument`, but no property-red task proves that the harness detects its characteristic false greens. The added owed tasks require the contract and at least controlled red cases before implementation. They do not expand the product scope or alter the correct “sim is not metal” boundary.
