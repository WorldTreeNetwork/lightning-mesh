## Comparison with r1

- **Process-to-well boundary resolved.** ADR decisions 3–5 and the delta's “Allowlisted stdout well” require fixed argv without a shell, combined stdout/stderr, visible non-zero status, finite one-shot execution and capture, and distinguishable loading, empty success, failure and truncation. Replacement and destruction cancel and reap; at most one child may run per canvas. Decision 5 explicitly rejects stale output. Unsupported hosts do not spawn. Concrete timeout/cap values and IPC types are assigned to `add-host-ifconfig-view`, as r1 allowed. The no-poll first landing is explicit. The generic refresh sentence at the end of Security does not create a polling obligation against decision 5 and the SHALL text.
- **Resize contradiction resolved.** ADR decision 6 and “Modifier move and resize” separate gesture selection from live modifiers. Option/Alt at pointer-down wins over Control and selects resize. During resize, held Option means center anchoring, released Option means fixed top-left, and live Shift preserves the initial aspect ratio. Control without Option selects move; later modifier changes cannot convert it. Scenarios cover precedence and both resize anchors.
- **Scope contradiction resolved.** ADR decision 8, the proposal, tasks and “One canvas in this landing” agree: one canvas plus host ifconfig is the fold gate. Additional simultaneous canvases and layout wait on `add-crystal-grid`; source switching waits on `add-view-swap`. No two-window acceptance scenario remains.

## Steelman against the independent take

A combined transcript is sufficient for this operator inspection; separate stderr panes and a structured NIC parser would add obligations without answering the stated need. Similarly, requiring numeric limits or a particular cancellation transport in this parent would duplicate the pipe child's work: the parent now pins the externally observable bounds and lifetime that both children need. Truncation marks incomplete capture; it does not waive the explicit failure rule for timeout or non-zero exit.

Copying the Papyrus chrome and using a separate overlay are proportionate to the requested owner-drawn interaction despite their native-platform cost. The main window retains discovery, radio apply and guild signing, matching the cited code and living contracts. The declared `canvas-chrome-port` spike is the existing empirical gate before `add-admin-canvas` act, rather than a reason to invent a different UI here.
