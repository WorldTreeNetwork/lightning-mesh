## Blind take (written before design.md / tasks.md / r1–r3 were opened)

Sources: proposal Why, steer.md, the spec delta, `roam.rs`
(`MOBILITY_ROUTE_PROTO`, `guest_routes`, flap guard), `mjolnir-meshd.rs`
`apply_mobility_route` / `flush_mobility_routes`, `babel/config.rs`
(`mobility_redistribute_line`, `in ... deny` lines), `crdt/leased_name.rs`,
`crdt/merge.rs` L170, `crdt/gossip.rs` L134, beads sz9 / 661, living spec
`identity-derived-ula`.

1. Pin: the sz9 carve-out is necessary, not optional. `redistribute proto 158 allow` exports a `/32` inside the *home* node's `/24` under the *visited* node's router-id; a literal origin-authorization filter drops it and inbound blackholes at home.
2. Pin: the carve-out must be bounded to `/32` in client space. `10.254`, `10.255`, `fc00::/7` are already `in ... deny`; `0.0.0.0/0` must stay behind the gateway grant. A carve-out worded as "host routes" without the space bound would let an insider `/32` a backhaul address.
3. Pin: the carve-out must not read as a `/24` grant. "V may originate `10.42.12.50/32`" must sit beside "V may not originate `10.42.12.0/24`" in the same scenario, or act collapses them.
4. Refuse: a signed mobility lane in this change. It changes sz9's design (kernel route, no CRDT), adds a lane to fyby.3, and the flap guard is a local liveness check, not something a signature improves.
5. Refuse: any carve-out that keys on the immediate neighbor. The `/32` transits jump nodes like every other prefix; the binding stays on router-id origin.
6. Tradeoff: enrolled-insider single-`/32` hijack. At house scale this is the same class as the S5 origin-forgery residual and strictly weaker than it (the S5 insider can already forge the whole `/24` under H's router-id). R5 revocation is the exit for both.
7. Concern: the highest-value `/32` in each subnet is the gateway host `10.42.X.1`. An insider `/32` on it hijacks that node's hello.mesh mesh-wide and breaks roamed clients' unicast DHCP renewals. Still inside the S5 residual, so a note, not a refusal.
8. Concern: "with a live session" is ambiguous between the origin and the delivering neighbor. For transit only the neighbor session can be live; the origin check is roster membership.
9. Concern: `leased_name` at HEAD signs `name+port+challenge` only; `ip`, `scheme`, and the HLC are outside the signature. It is a wire precedent for the *tag* shape, not for the S4 canonical-bytes content. The design must not let act copy its coverage.
10. Tradeoff to accept: two honest nodes briefly both announcing one `/32` during a roam. Pre-existing sz9 limit; 661 freshness does not change it and should not try.

## After opening design.md, tasks.md, r1–r3

### r3 items: answered

- **S6 sz9 carve-out (option a).** Delta 661 requirement now reads
  "except mobility host `/32`s (sz9, proto 158): a `/32` in the mesh
  client space originated by any enrolled router-id with a live session
  SHALL still install." Scenario "Roamed client /32 from an enrolled
  visited node still installs" is present with the "does not authorize V
  to originate H's whole `/24`" arm. Design pin names the single-`/32`
  hijack as R5 residual, disclaims a signed mobility lane, and repeats
  "do not drop `pub mod roam`". Matches take 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. The
  acceptance contrast's if_true clause holds: origin binding no longer
  blackholes roam.
- **N10 canonical bytes.** Design pins a versioned, domain-tagged
  explicit message per lane and mutation type, not postcard of the
  struct. Answered; also covers take 9's content risk as long as act
  reads "per lane and mutation type" as "lane key + HLC + fields",
  which the S4 requirement text already says.
- **N11 frozen variants.** Design pins decode-and-drop at merge once
  fyby.3 is enforced. Answered.
- **N15 leased_name.** Design now pins "wire precedent, not proof that
  HEAD re-verifies at merge (it does not)". Answered at the level of
  intent; see N16 for the leftover.

### Notes (not blocking, for act)

- **N16 design.md self-contradiction.** The "CRDT authz is subject
  signatures" pin still says "(`leased_name` already does this)" while
  the r3 pin two screens down says HEAD does not re-verify at merge.
  Delete the parenthetical when next touching design.md. An implementer
  who reads only the first pin will think fyby.3 is half done.
- **N17 "live session" wording.** Take 8. Read as: the update arrives
  over a live authenticated neighbor session AND the origin router-id is
  in the enrolled roster. That is the same reading S5 already needs for
  jump re-announce of a `/24`; one clause in the 661 requirement would
  save act a question.
- **N18 gateway host `/32`.** Take 7. Honest-node hygiene: `guest_routes`
  already skips the own subnet and only emits ARP-reachable, associated
  MACs, so an honest node never emits another node's `.1`. A receiver-side
  exclusion of claimed gateway hosts from the carve-out is cheap defense
  in depth against a buggy (not malicious) node and costs nothing for
  roam. Optional; the malicious case is S5 residual regardless.
- **N19 leased_name coverage.** Take 9. When the name lane is re-signed
  under S4, the canonical bytes must add the lane key (name), the HLC,
  and either cover `ip`/`scheme` or keep them explicitly vouched-by-
  ingester. Do not inherit `mjolnir-name-claim:v1`'s field set.
- **N12–N14 from r3** (router-id derivation and §9 `random-id true`,
  per-claim `in` filter churn proven on babeld 1.13 hardware, revocation
  evicting persisted claims) remain open notes. None is a spec hole;
  each is a thing act's 661 / fyby.3 packet should carry.
